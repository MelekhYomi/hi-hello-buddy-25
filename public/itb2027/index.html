Who it is for

This is for you if…

You have a business idea but do not know where to start.
You have started selling, but people do not understand or remember your brand.
You are spending money on a logo or adverts before you are clear about your customer.
You want a realistic plan, not a motivational speech.

Already running a business? Use each step to find the weakest part of your foundation, rather than starting from zero.

The problem

Most ideas do not fail for lack of passion

No real problem

The idea was never tied to a customer problem someone will pay to solve.

Never tested

Nobody checked whether people would actually buy before money was spent.

Numbers skipped

Costs, price and break-even were guessed, so a busy business still loses money.

Brand = logo

The brand stopped at a picture, with no promise, voice or customer experience behind it.

Idea to Brand takes you through all four, step by step, in one day.

How the day works

Learn it. See it. Do it. Share it.

Every module follows the same rhythm, so you always know what is expected.

Learn

A short idea with one key question and no jargon.

See

A worked example from a lunch-pack seller in Jos, so the idea is concrete.

Do

You write in your own workbook, on your own idea.

Share

A few minutes with a partner to say it out loud and sharpen it.

You will practise a customer interview, work out your pricing and break-even point, write your brand promise and plan your first 30 days.

Your participant workbook

Room to write. Pages to keep.

Every attendee gets a printed A4 workbook. Fill it in during the session and keep it as your plan.

  • An exercise for every step, each with a time limit.
  • A worked example beside every exercise, so you can see what a good answer looks like.
  • Ruled writing space for handwritten answers.
  • Checklists for registration, your brand and AI use.
  • A one-page summary of your business idea to finish at home.
